What ILAI can do

Less time on paperwork. More time on the work.

Every ILAI capability delivers a result: documents handled, quotes prepared, processes running on their own — on your machine, with your data. Here's what it does for you.

🧠

Understands context

It works on documents, procedures, and internal resources, so the answers are not generic. It reads what matters to you.

🛠️

Runs real workflows

It can follow instructions, coordinate repetitive tasks, and complete operational work. Not just talk.

🌱

Grows with use

Every configuration, process, and rule stays with you. The system becomes more useful over time.

🔒

Data always on site

Documents, personal data, and know-how stay on your network. No data leaves, no data is trained on public models.

⚡

Instant responses

No cloud latency, no network bottleneck. Works immediately, even when the connection is down.

📈

Accumulating value

Every completed task leaves a trace. Over time, ILAI knows your work better than anyone else.

How it works

First you configure it. Then you put it to work.

1

The machine, the mailbox, the permissions.

A dedicated environment with a clear perimeter and controlled access. Nothing leaves your network.

2

You onboard it like a new colleague.

Who you are, what the company does, where it finds the right documents. Then it reads and learns your context.

3

You assign, review, and iterate.

Good work is done. Work that needs fixing gets fixed, and the system improves. The knowledge stays on your machine.

Documents that stay inside

Contracts, quotes, case files, internal documentation: it reads, organizes, compares, and drafts without moving a single byte off-site.
