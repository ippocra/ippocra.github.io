The problem

Your AI doesn't know your business.

You might have one AI subscription. Or two. Or one per department. The problem isn't the number of subscriptions: it's that none of those systems know your business. They don't know who your clients are. They don't know your pricing. They don't touch your ERP. Every time you use them, you have to explain who you are.

This is true for a 3-person company just as much as a 300-person one. The gap is always the same: your AI is a generalist that knows nothing about you.
What ILAI is

A colleague. Not a tool.

ILAI is an AI colleague trained on your business. Your documents, your workflows, your clients, your systems. You use it in the channels you already have — Slack, WhatsApp, email, Teams — and it already knows who you are, what you do, and how you work.

You don't need more AI subscriptions. You need one that actually knows your business.

In practice

These are the first jobs you put it to. The list isn't closed: if there's a workflow that requires business context and documents, ILAI can work on it.

📥

Client intake & orders

A request comes in: ILAI classifies it, checks the client history, drafts the response, creates the order in the ERP. You approve, it executes.

📋

Quotes & proposals

Pulls product data, checks your pricing rules, drafts the quote, flags what needs your approval. Less time on paperwork, more time on the client.

Marketing & content

Drafts content in your voice, against your brand guidelines. Plans campaigns based on what you've done before, tracks execution.

ERP integration

Not just documents: it works with your systems.

ILAI doesn't just read and write documents. It integrates with the ERP you already use: reads data (orders, clients, products, pricing) and writes back (new order created, status updated, flag set). We build the integration, for the system you actually use. You don't write a line of code.

Reads from your ERP. Open orders, clients, product data, pricing rules, history. ILAI has the full context without you copying it in.
Writes to your ERP. New order created, status updated, flag set, note added. The work is done in the system, not in a separate file.
We build the integration. We need to understand your ERP and build the bridge. You keep working the way you do — just with one more colleague.
