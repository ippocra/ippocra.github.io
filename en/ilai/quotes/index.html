How it works

You talk to ILAI like a colleague.

1

You send the request.

The way you'd send it to an employee, by email, WhatsApp or voice: "Make a quote for Mario Rossi: heat pump installation, 120sqm flat".

2

ILAI asks the questions that matter.

"What output? Do you already have a pump? What kind of system is there now?" The same questions your own technician would ask.

3

It consults the company data.

Materials price list, labor rates, overhead, margin — all from your configuration. No prices from memory.

4

You approve or edit.

ILAI presents the total: "€3,947.16 — approve or change?" You change a line, everything recalculates.

5

ILAI sends it to the client.

The clean, lump-sum one goes to the client. The internal one stays private to you. Done.

What it costs

No fixed price, and that's the point. Every company has its own materials price list, its own hourly rates, its own margins and overhead. Connecting them requires a custom onboarding effort, built for your business — not the same box sold to everyone.
The onboarding quote is built with you. It depends on how many price-list and labor items we need to connect and how structured your way of working is. In the first call we tell you exactly what it includes and what it costs.

This isn't a subscription to a generic quoting software. It's a colleague that learns your price list, your rates and your margins — and then writes the quotes for you, in minutes, without errors.

Frequently asked questions

The things you always ask.

Why is there no fixed price?

Because every company has its own data: materials price list, hourly rates, margins, overhead. Integrating them requires a custom onboarding effort, built for your business. We don't sell the same box to everyone: the onboarding quote is built together with you, based on how many price-list and labor items we need to connect.

Does my company data leave?

The price list, the rates and the margins stay yours. You can run ILAI locally, where the machine, the data and the know-how stay inside your own network; or use the cloud version on Italian infrastructure with Zero Data Retention. Either way, the prices you use for quotes don't become anyone else's.

How fast is a quote ready?

As a rule in 5 minutes. It depends on the complexity of the job, but the rule is: you send the request the way you'd send it to an employee, and the quote — internal and client-facing — comes back ready for approval.

Can I approve and edit before sending?

Yes. ILAI presents the quote with the total and you say "approve" or "change this". Only when you approve it is it sent to the client. The internal version stays private to you; the client gets the clean, lump-sum one.

Does it work for my trade?

Yes. In the course with Confartigianato we demonstrated it on plumbing and auto workshops, with real quotes (heat pump, boiler, service). If your trade has a price list and some rates, ILAI can work with it.
