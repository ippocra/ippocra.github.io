Which contract

The three types we see most often.

📦

Supply contract

You buy from a supplier: late penalties, liability for defects, termination, price changes. Standard clauses often cost more than you think.

🏗️

Subcontracting contract

You outsource work to a contractor: deadlines, penalties, intellectual property, subcontracting. The clauses that protect you are not always the ones your partner inserted.

👥

Service / collaboration contract

Freelance, consultants, collaborators: scope, exclusivity, non-compete, ownership of the work. Exclusivity and non-compete clauses are the most expensive to get wrong.
