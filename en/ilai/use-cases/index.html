High-value workflows

Where ILAI can start working.

📄

Confidential documents

Contracts, quotes, case files, and internal documentation: ILAI reads, organizes, compares, and drafts without moving data off-site.

🧭

Operational procedures

Turn checklists, manuals, and scattered instructions into a colleague that can follow the process and leave a trace of the work done.

✉️

Email and recurring requests

Triage requests, draft replies, retrieve attachments, and keep context across email, chat, and company documents.

📊

Reports and monitoring

Collect data from files, dashboards, and internal sources to produce periodic reports with issues, signals, and recommended actions.

🤝

Team support

Respond in the channels you already use, coordinate repetitive tasks, and reduce the time lost searching for information.

🔒

Sensitive contexts

When privacy, know-how, or data control are part of the competitive edge, the AI should stay inside your infrastructure.
