How you work together

First you configure it. Then you put it to work.

1

You give it its machine, mailbox, and permissions.

We set up a dedicated environment with a clear perimeter and controlled access.

2

You onboard it like a new colleague.

You explain who you are, what your company does, and where it can find the right resources to do the job.

3

You assign a task, review the outcome, and iterate.

If the job is good, it is done. If it needs work, you correct it and improve it.
