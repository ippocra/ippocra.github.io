Why a dedicated machine in the cloud

The same product. Without buying anything.

⬡

It's yours, not shared

Each unit is dedicated to a single customer: the same "your machine" story as on-prem ILAI, just in the cloud. No shared infrastructure, no multi-tenant setup.

💳

Zero hardware to buy

No upfront investment, no waiting for a workstation to be delivered. You start with a monthly fee — no capex.

⚡

You start immediately

We provision and configure it for your case. Onboarding is identical to ILAI on-prem: you tell it who you are and where it finds its resources.

Privacy, said plainly

Your data leaves the office. It stays in Italy. And it is not kept.

Unlike on-prem ILAI, in the cloud your documents travel outside your network. We say that out loud — and here are the three guarantees that protect that journey.

🇮🇹

Italian data center

Cloud units run on Seeweb infrastructure, in Italy. Your documents never leave the country: they stay under Italian and European jurisdiction.

🔒

Dedicated machine

Your unit is yours: no other customer shares your machine, your documents, or your context. It's on-prem ILAI, hosted by us.

Inference certified Zero Data Retention (Regolo). The inference engine processes every request in memory and discards it definitively: no storage of prompts and responses, no request logging, no reuse for training. The certification was issued following an independent third-party audit (July 2026). View the certification →

If "your data never leaves the country — or your network" is an absolute rule for you, the right choice remains on-prem ILAI: the machine, your documents, and the know-how all stay inside your network.

Three steps. We manage the machine.

Monthly fee, no hardware

You pay a fee. You buy nothing.

Monthly fee per unit. No hardware investment, no pay-as-you-go costs. The fee starts at €350/month for a dedicated unit, depending on model and workload: we confirm the final number together in the first call. This is a starting figure — the final price depends on your configuration.

This is also home to ILAI Contracts: the contract-specialized version runs in the cloud, because contracts already travel to suppliers and partners. €250/month, report in 20 minutes.

When it makes sense, move it home. Your unit — everything it has learned — can migrate from cloud to on-prem. This is the path we take most often: start in the cloud, and when the AI has proven useful, bring it home.
