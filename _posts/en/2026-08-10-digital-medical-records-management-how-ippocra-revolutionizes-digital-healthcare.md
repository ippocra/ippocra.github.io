**Have you ever tried to find a medical report among a pile of paper, a safety deposit box, and who knows where else?**

If you're a patient, you know all too well: reports accumulate, get lost, deteriorate. And when you need them — for a specialist visit, a second opinion, an emergency — they're somewhere, but you don't know where.

If you're a doctor or healthcare professional, the problem is even more complex: you need access to data that isn't in your facility, produced by different laboratories, in different formats, with different delivery times.

## The problem of medical reports in Italian digital healthcare

Italy has made enormous strides in healthcare digitalization. The Electronic Health Record (Fascicolo Sanitario Elettronico) exists, regions have computerized systems, laboratories produce digital reports. Yet for the average patient, managing their own reports is a frustrating experience:

- **Reports are scattered**. Each healthcare facility keeps them separate
- **Formats differ**. PDFs, images, DICOM for imaging exams — nothing unified
- **Sharing is complicated**. Taking a report from one specialist to another requires time and often information loss
- **Access is fragmented**. Each region has its own EHR, each facility has its own portal

Think about your grandmother. Or your parents. Or yourself. How many times have you had to restart a visit because you couldn't find a report?

## Ippo: The digital health platform that puts the patient first

We built [Ippo](https://ippocra.com/ippo) with a simple idea: **health data belongs to the patient, not to the facilities**.

Here's what Ippo does:

### 1. Unifies all your reports in one place

Whether the report is a PDF, an image, or a DICOM file for an imaging analysis. Ippo imports them all, organizes by person, and makes them accessible from any device.

### 2. Organizes them automatically

You don't need to catalog manually. Ippo reads the reports, identifies the document type, determines which person it belongs to, and places it in the right folder. It works like an assistant that organizes things for you.

### 3. Makes them shareable with one click

When a doctor needs your reports, you don't have to print, scan, or carry them physically. With IppoLink — Ippocra's secure sharing system — you can send heavy reports (even CT scans or MRIs) encrypted and traceable. The recipient accesses, views, and downloads. Done.

### 4. Preserves them forever

Digital reports are easily lost: a broken hard drive, an expired account, a facility change. With Ippo, your reports are yours. They're saved securely and remain accessible forever, even if you change doctors, facilities, or regions.

## Why digital healthcare needs Ippo

The current digital healthcare model is facility-centric: each hospital, each lab, each health authority manages its data in isolation. The patient is a passive subject who must physically go or call to obtain what belongs to them.

Ippo flips this model:

- **The patient is at the center**. Your data, your control, your choice
- **Technology is invisible**. You don't need to learn a new tool — it just works
- **Security is guaranteed**. End-to-end encryption, no data shared without your explicit consent
- **Accessibility is universal**. Works on any device, anytime

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Ippo free?**
Ippo offers a free plan for individual patients. For healthcare facilities and professionals, there are dedicated plans with advanced features.

**Are my health data safe?**
Yes. Ippo uses end-to-end encryption for all documents. Data is stored on secure servers and is never shared without your explicit consent.

**Can I use Ippo with the Electronic Health Record (FSE)?**
Yes. Ippo is designed to integrate with existing systems, including Regional Electronic Health Records. It doesn't replace the FSE — it enhances it.

**How does IppoLink work?**
IppoLink is a secure sharing system that allows you to send heavy health documents (even several GB) via a temporary encrypted link. The recipient accesses, views, and downloads the documents. You control who can access and for how long.

**Is Ippo available in all Italian regions?**
Yes. Ippo is not tied to any specific region. It works throughout Italy and can be used with data from any healthcare facility.
