**Artificial intelligence is changing the way companies work.** But there's a massive problem that few discuss: when you use cloud AI, you're essentially renting your intelligence to someone else.

For Italian SMEs, this isn't just a technical issue — it's a business survival issue.

## The problem with cloud AI for SMEs

Think about your company. Your commercial documents, your pricing, your contacts, your strategies — these are all data you'd never want to see on third-party servers. Yet every time you use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other cloud AI, that data becomes part of an ecosystem you don't control.

Here's what really happens when you use cloud AI:

- **Your data is no longer yours.** Once sent to a cloud service, you lose control over what happens to it
- **Costs aren't predictable.** Usage-based models grow linearly: the more you use AI, the more you pay. And when your company grows, costs explode
- **Dependency is total.** If the cloud service changes price, interrupts service, or changes policy, your company is stuck
- **Compliance is at risk.** GDPR, data privacy regulations, industry-specific rules — everything becomes more complex when data leaves your company

## What is local artificial intelligence?

Local artificial intelligence is exactly what it sounds like: an AI that runs **on your infrastructure**, not on remote servers.

With local AI:

1. **Your data stays yours.** All processing happens within your company's network
2. **Costs are fixed.** You buy the infrastructure once, then it runs for years with no recurring costs
3. **You don't depend on anyone.** No price changes, no service interruptions, no external policies
4. **Compliance is simple.** Data never leaves your company — GDPR is satisfied by design

## Why Italian SMEs should consider local AI

Italy has over 4 million SMEs. Many of these handle sensitive data — contractual, financial, personal — that cannot be shared with cloud services.

### Sectors that benefit the most

**Legal firms.** A lawyer working with confidential documents, cases, trade secrets — no cloud can guarantee those documents are never processed by an external model.

**Accounting firms.** Balance sheets, tax filings, client data — every year sensitive data grows. Keeping it local is the only reasonable choice.

**Medical practices.** Health data is the most sensitive of all. GDPR protects it particularly. Local AI is the only option that fully complies with the regulation.

**Artisan manufacturing.** Production formulas, designs, supplier relationships — all material that defines a company's competitive advantage.

**Consulting and marketing.** Commercial strategies, development plans, client data — the raw material of these activities is knowledge, and knowledge shouldn't leave the company.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Is local AI more expensive than cloud AI?**
No. Cloud AI has seemingly low costs at first, but they grow linearly with usage. Local AI has an initial investment (hardware + setup) but then runs with no recurring costs. Over the long term, local AI is almost always more economical.

**Do I need a powerful computer to run local AI?**
You need adequate infrastructure, but not a supercomputer. A workstation with a dedicated GPU (roughly €3,000-€7,000) is sufficient to run professional-grade artificial intelligence models.

**Is local AI less powerful than cloud AI?**
It's not about raw power — it's about approach. Cloud AI is a generic model trying to please everyone. Local AI is customized to your business context, so it's much more useful and accurate for your specific needs.

**Can I use both local AI and cloud AI together?**
Absolutely. Many companies use both: cloud AI for general use and local AI for sensitive data and critical processes. The choice isn't "all or nothing."
