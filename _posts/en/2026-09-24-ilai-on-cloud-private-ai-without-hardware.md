ILAI remains one product, with two ways to deploy it: on the company’s own machine or in the cloud, on a unit dedicated to that customer. The right choice depends on where the business is today, its constraints, and how it wants to begin.

## The question is not whether to use AI

Many businesses already know where an AI colleague could help: documents to analyse, procedures to follow, repetitive requests, internal information to retrieve, and tasks that consume time without creating proportional value.

The transition from curiosity to daily use can still be slowed by a practical question: **where should the AI run?**

On-premise deployment remains the clearest choice for a company that wants processing inside its own network. But it requires a dedicated machine, suitable space, and an initial investment. For some businesses, that is the ideal solution. For others, it is a step that comes after the value of the project has been demonstrated.

ILAI on Cloud removes that initial obstacle.

## What is ILAI on Cloud?

ILAI on Cloud is a private ILAI instance hosted on a **unit dedicated to each customer**. It is not a generic chatbot shared with other businesses, and it is not simply a subscription to a public AI model.

It follows the same ILAI approach, with a different infrastructure model:

- an AI colleague configured around the company’s context;
- a private unit, not shared between customers;
- a monthly subscription model;
- infrastructure hosted in Italy;
- the ability to start without buying dedicated hardware immediately.

Interested businesses can [learn about ILAI on Cloud and join the waitlist](https://ilai.ippocra.com/en/cloud/).

## Seeweb and Regolo: what “private” means in the cloud

The word “cloud” should not hide where data is processed or what technical safeguards are in place.

ILAI on Cloud uses Italian infrastructure provided by [Seeweb](https://www.seeweb.it/), with a dedicated unit for each customer. Inference uses [Regolo’s Zero Data Retention-certified infrastructure](https://regolo.ai/zero-data-retention/): within the scope of that certification, data sent for processing is not stored, logged, or reused.

The wording needs to be precise: **with ILAI on Cloud, data leaves the company’s premises but remains in Italy**. This is the essential difference from on-premise deployment, where both data and processing remain inside the customer’s network.

The infrastructure location and Zero Data Retention are important elements, but they do not replace a complete security and compliance assessment for each company’s specific context.

## Two deployment modes, the same ILAI

| Aspect | ILAI on-premise | ILAI on Cloud |
|---|---|---|
| Where it runs | On the company’s own machine | On a dedicated unit in Italy |
| Data | Stays inside the company network | Leaves the premises but stays in Italy |
| Hardware | Purchased and installed by the customer | Managed as part of the cloud service |
| Costs | Initial investment | Monthly subscription |
| Getting started | Requires infrastructure preparation | Start without buying hardware |
| Future path | Already the local destination | Can evolve into on-premise deployment |

There is no universally correct choice. There is the deployment model that best fits where the business is today.

## Why start in the cloud?

ILAI on Cloud may be the right starting point for a business that:

- wants to test a concrete use case without taking on the hardware investment immediately;
- wants to start quickly with a focused process;
- does not yet have the infrastructure required to run AI locally;
- wants to validate the value of AI before choosing a final configuration;
- has distributed teams or people who need secure access from different locations;
- wants to turn a pilot project into an operational tool through a gradual path.

The goal is not to buy technology for its own sake. The goal is to understand where an AI colleague can remove repetitive work, make internal information easier to use, and help the team work better.

## From cloud to the company’s own machine

The cloud does not have to become a permanent dependency. For many businesses, it can be the simplest way to start, learn, and measure the value of ILAI.

Once the role of the AI colleague is clear, the company can decide to move to a dedicated machine at its own premises. The cloud-to-on-premise path is therefore part of the project from the beginning, not an unexpected migration to deal with later.

This approach separates two decisions that are often confused:

1. understanding whether AI is genuinely useful for a business process;
2. choosing where that AI should ultimately be hosted.

ILAI on Cloud lets a company answer the first question before making the second decision, without giving up the option of on-premise deployment later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is ILAI on Cloud a standard SaaS product?**

No. ILAI on Cloud is based on a unit dedicated to a single customer. It is not a shared environment where different businesses use the same generic AI colleague.

**Where is the infrastructure located?**

The cloud infrastructure is hosted in Italy on Seeweb infrastructure. Inference uses Regolo Zero Data Retention-certified infrastructure.

**Does the data stay inside my company?**

With ILAI on Cloud, data leaves the company’s premises to be processed on its dedicated unit, but remains in Italy. With ILAI on-premise, both data and processing stay inside the company network.

**Can I start in the cloud and move to on-premise later?**

Yes. Moving from the cloud to the company’s own machine is an intended path for businesses that want to start quickly and move the deployment on-site after proving the value of the project.

**How much does ILAI on Cloud cost?**

The service uses a monthly subscription. Pricing depends on the context and configuration path and is discussed with interested businesses through the waitlist.
