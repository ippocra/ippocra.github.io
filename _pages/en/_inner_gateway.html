The belief

The most reliable technology is the kind that asks nothing of you.

The information that defines us — the documents we produce, the data we generate, the decisions we make — belongs to the people who create it. Not to platforms. Not to vendors. Not to whoever holds the API key.

So we build the other way around. Systems that run where your data lives, that you own outright, that stay transparent about what they do. When control stays with you, technology stops being a risk and becomes an asset.

How we build

Three commitments, every product

Private by design

Sensitive data never has to leave your network. No third-party inference, no hidden dependencies, no trust assumptions. If your data is private, it stays that way — by architecture, not by promise.

Local by default

Software that runs where it's needed, on hardware you control. Predictable costs, zero lock-in, full sovereignty. You can migrate, fork, or switch without a single hostage taken.

Agency, always

We build for the people who use the software — not for the platforms that resell it. Every system we ship is legible, auditable, and under your command. That's what we mean by agency.

The lab

We don't ship and stop.

Ippocra runs like a research lab, not a product factory. We prototype in public, benchmark relentlessly, and publish what we learn — because the fastest way to build the next decade's tools is to keep asking what the current ones get wrong.

Every product is a step along a longer line of experiments. When one matures enough to carry real work, it ships. The rest stays in the lab, pushing the limits of what local, private intelligence can do.
