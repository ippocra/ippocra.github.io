The Architecture

Four architectural principles. Not features — constraints that shape every decision.

Principle 01
Skill-Based AI
AI capabilities as discrete, composable skills. Each skill has a defined input, output, and scope. Testable, auditable, deployable independently. The pattern that makes AI viable in regulated environments.
Principle 02
Local-First, Data Sovereignty
Systems run where the data lives. On your hardware, in your network. No inference to third-party services. No data egress. No telemetry. Private by architecture, not by promise.
Principle 03
Multi-Profile, Persistent Memory
Each user or context operates within its own profile — its own memory, context, and accumulated knowledge. Isolated by default. Persistent across sessions. Maps to multi-tenant enterprise.
Principle 04
Agentic Workflows
Skills are not just queried — they are orchestrated. Multi-step workflows: read, decide, act, report. From "chat with your data" to "your data works for you."
