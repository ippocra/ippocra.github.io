## 4. The Architecture

Ippocra's systems are built on four architectural principles. These are not features. They are constraints that shape every decision we make.

**Skill-Based AI**
AI capabilities are implemented as discrete, composable skills — not as a single general-purpose LLM with a prompt. Each skill has a defined input, a defined output, and a defined scope. Skills can be tested, audited, and deployed independently. This is the pattern that makes AI integration viable in regulated environments: you don't deploy "AI", you deploy a specific capability with a specific boundary.

**Local-First, Data Sovereignty**
Systems run where the data lives. On your hardware, in your network. No inference calls to third-party services. No data egress. No telemetry. If your data is sensitive, it stays sensitive — by architecture, not by promise. This is a requirement, not a differentiator, for any enterprise operating in regulated domains.

**Multi-Profile, Persistent Memory**
Each user or system context operates within its own profile — its own memory, its own context, its own accumulated knowledge. Profiles are isolated by default. Memory is persistent across sessions, meaning the system becomes more useful over time without external state management. This maps naturally to multi-tenant enterprise deployments where different teams or roles need different contexts.

**Agentic Workflows**
Skills are not just queried. They are orchestrated. Ippocra's systems execute multi-step workflows: they read, decide, act, and report. A workflow is a sequence of skill invocations with defined transitions. This is how AI moves from "chat with your data" to "your data works for you".

## 5. Core Capabilities

**Document Intelligence**
Pipeline of extraction that transforms real, chaotic documentation into structured, interrogable context. Contracts, reports, technical specifications, operational documents — ingested, parsed, and made queryable without leaving your network.

**Local LLM Orchestration**
Routing of sensitive workloads to the smallest capable local model, on-premise. No cloud dependency. No per-token billing. Predictable cost. The model selection layer is abstracted: your system gets the right capability for the task, not the most expensive model available.

**AI Agent Design & Integration**
Design and deployment of AI agents for specific business domains. An agent is a persistent, context-aware system that executes workflows autonomously within a defined scope. We design the skill set, the memory model, the workflow orchestration, and the integration layer.

**Enterprise AI Architecture Consulting**
Advisory for organisations evaluating AI integration in regulated or data-sensitive environments. We help define the architectural boundaries: what runs locally, what runs in a private cloud, what never leaves the perimeter. We map capabilities to compliance requirements. We design the integration pattern before a single line of code is written.

**Persistent Memory Systems**
Long-term memory architectures for AI systems that need to accumulate context over time. Memory is structured, queryable, and owned by the client. Not a vector database. Not a cache. A designed, auditable memory model that grows with the system.

**Multi-Tenant AI Deployment**
Deployment patterns for AI systems serving multiple users, teams, or organisations within the same infrastructure. Profile isolation, permission boundaries, context separation, and per-tenant memory management. Designed for enterprise environments where a single AI deployment must serve different stakeholders with different data access.

## 6. ILAI

**Ippocra Local Artificial Intelligence.**

ILAI is our flagship product: a local, on-premise AI system that operates as a persistent, skill-based assistant within your organisation. It runs on your hardware, connects to your existing communication channels (Slack, Teams, email, WhatsApp, Telegram), and executes workflows on your documents and data.

**How it works:**

1. **Onboarding** — you configure the system with your context: who you are, what your organisation does, where resources and documents live.
2. **Execution** — you assign a specific task. ILAI executes the workflow, produces the result, and reports. You review and correct if needed.
3. **Growth** — every interaction leaves knowledge on your machine. Over time, ILAI becomes more useful. The system is an asset, not a service.

**Deployment models:**

- **Local** — runs on your own workstation (3,000–7,000 € + VAT for the hardware). Full sovereignty. Zero cloud dependency.
- **Private Cloud** — runs in a dedicated Italian data center (Seeweb) with certified Zero Data Retention inference. Monthly fee from 350 €/month. No hardware. Immediate start.

**Two modes, one product:**

- **ILAI Local** — for organisations with sensitive documents and internal workflows.
- **ILAI Specialised** — domain-specific deployments (e.g., contract analysis) with dedicated skill sets.
