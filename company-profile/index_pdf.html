The Architecture

Ippocra’s systems are built on four architectural principles. These are not features. They are constraints that shape every decision we make.

Skill-Based AI

AI capabilities are implemented as discrete, composable skills — not as a single general-purpose LLM with a prompt. Each skill has a defined input, a defined output, and a defined scope. Skills can be tested, audited, and deployed independently. This is the pattern that makes AI integration viable in regulated environments.

Local-First, Data Sovereignty

Systems run where the data lives. On your hardware, in your network. No inference calls to third-party services. No data egress. No telemetry. If your data is sensitive, it stays sensitive — by architecture, not by promise.

Multi-Profile, Persistent Memory

Each user or system context operates within its own profile — its own memory, its own context, its own accumulated knowledge. Profiles are isolated by default. Memory is persistent across sessions. This maps naturally to multi-tenant enterprise deployments.

Agentic Workflows

Skills are not just queried. They are orchestrated. Ippocra’s systems execute multi-step workflows: they read, decide, act, and report. This is how AI moves from “chat with your data” to “your data works for you”.

Core Capabilities

Document Intelligence

Pipeline of extraction that transforms real, chaotic documentation into structured, interrogable context. Contracts, reports, technical specifications, operational documents — ingested, parsed, and made queryable without leaving your network.

Local LLM Orchestration

Routing of sensitive workloads to the smallest capable local model, on-premise. No cloud dependency. No per-token billing. Predictable cost. The model selection layer is abstracted: your system gets the right capability for the task, not the most expensive model available.

AI Agent Design & Integration

Design and deployment of AI agents for specific business domains. An agent is a persistent, context-aware system that executes workflows autonomously within a defined scope. We design the skill set, the memory model, the workflow orchestration, and the integration layer.

Enterprise AI Architecture Consulting

Advisory for organisations evaluating AI integration in regulated or data-sensitive environments. We help define the architectural boundaries: what runs locally, what runs in a private cloud, what never leaves the perimeter. We map capabilities to compliance requirements.

Persistent Memory Systems

Long-term memory architectures for AI systems that need to accumulate context over time. Memory is structured, queryable, and owned by the client. Not a vector database. Not a cache. A designed, auditable memory model that grows with the system.

Multi-Tenant AI Deployment

Deployment patterns for AI systems serving multiple users, teams, or organisations within the same infrastructure. Profile isolation, permission boundaries, context separation, and per-tenant memory management.

ILAI

Ippocra Local Artificial Intelligence.

ILAI is our flagship product: a local, on-premise AI system that operates as a persistent, skill-based assistant within your organisation. 1

You configure the system with your context: who you are, what your organisation does, where resources and documents live.

3

Every interaction leaves knowledge on your machine. Over time, ILAI becomes more useful. The system is an asset, not a service.

Local

Private Cloud

Dedicated infrastructure with certified Zero Data Retention inference. No hardware on your side. Immediate start.

The Research Lab

Ippocra operates as a research lab, not a product factory. We prototype in public, benchmark obsessively, and publish what we learn. Every product is a step along a longer line of experiments. When one matures enough to support real work, it goes to production. The rest stays in the lab, pushing the limits of what local, private intelligence can do.
